## Biological Interpretation

CYP2C19 is involved in drug metabolism. Genetic variants can affect enzyme function and may influence how individuals respond to certain medications.

However, a computational prediction alone is not enough to establish clinical significance. Clinical interpretation requires reliable supporting evidence.
